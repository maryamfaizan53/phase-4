🚀 Beyond the To-Do List: Announcing a New Era of Productivity! 🚀

Are your to-do lists feeling a bit... static? What if you could transform your daily tasks into actionable insights?

I'm thrilled to unveil a project that redefines personal productivity: a simple to-do app, supercharged with a SaaS-style analytics dashboard. This isn't just about checking boxes; it's about understanding your workflow and optimizing your performance.

✨ **Key Features:**
- **At-a-Glance KPIs:** Instantly see your total, completed, pending, and overdue tasks.
- **Rich Data Visualizations:** Interactive donut, line, and bar charts bring your data to life, showing you trends and patterns in your productivity.
- **Advanced Task Management:** A powerful table with full CRUD, real-time search, filters, and pagination.
- **Sleek & Modern UI:** A beautiful glassmorphism design that's both professional and a joy to use.

🤖 **A Unique Development Story:**
This project was built using a cutting-edge Spec-Driven Development (SDD) methodology, with a little help from an AI assistant (Claude Sonnet 4.5). This collaborative approach between human and machine allowed for rapid, high-quality development, from spec to production-ready MVP.

🔧 **Tech Stack:**
- **Frontend:** Next.js 14, React, Tailwind CSS, Recharts
- **Backend:** FastAPI, Python
- **Database:** PostgreSQL

This project is a testament to the power of modern web technologies and the potential of AI-assisted development to build powerful applications, faster.
